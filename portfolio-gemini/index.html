FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before hiring me.

Do you work with clients outside Tamil Nadu? +

Yes! I work with clients across India — remotely via WhatsApp, email, and video call. Payment via UPI, bank transfer, or Razorpay.

How does the payment work? +

50% advance to start the project, remaining 50% on final delivery before domain/hosting handover. Payment via UPI/bank transfer.

What if I'm not happy with the design? +

Every project includes 2 free revision rounds. I share previews during development so you can give feedback early. Additional revisions can be done for a small fee.

Do you provide hosting and domain? +

I guide you to purchase your own domain and hosting (recommended for ownership). I handle setup and deployment. Hosting recommendations: Hostinger (~₹99/mo), Vercel (free for basic sites).

What tech stack will you use? +

For business websites: HTML/CSS/React. For web apps: MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) or Java Spring Boot. For design: Figma. The best stack is chosen based on your requirements.

Can you maintain my website after delivery? +

Yes! I offer monthly maintenance packages starting at ₹2,000/month. This covers content updates, bug fixes, and small improvements.
